About Adventure Hunt Treks

Adventure Hunt Treks Pvt. Ltd. is a government-registered trekking and tour operator based in Suryabinayak, Bhaktapur, Nepal. We specialize in providing unforgettable adventure experiences across Nepal, from the majestic Himalayas to the lush jungles of Chitwan.

Whether you're a Nepali looking to explore your own country or a foreign visitor discovering Nepal for the first time, we have the perfect package for you.
